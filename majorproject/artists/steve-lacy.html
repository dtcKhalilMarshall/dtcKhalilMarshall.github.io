Steve Lacy

Steve Lacy is an American musician, singer, songwriter, and record producer. Known for his innovative blend of R&B, funk, indie rock, and soul, he first gained fame as a member of The Internet. His solo work showcases his unique style, combining soulful melodies with experimental sounds, making him a significant figure in contemporary Black music.
