Music Genres

Pop

Pop is a genre characterized by catchy melodies and hooks.

Hip-Hop

Hip-Hop features rhythmic speech and beats, often with poetic lyrics.

R&B

Rhythm and Blues focuses on soulful vocals and groove-oriented music.

Rock

Rock features electric guitars, drums, and energetic performances.

Electronic

Electronic music uses synthesizers and digital production techniques.

Jazz

Jazz is known for improvisation, complex chords, and swing rhythms.

Classical

Classical music involves intricate compositions, orchestras, and traditional instruments.

Reggae

Reggae features offbeat rhythms and socially conscious lyrics originating from Jamaica.

Country

Country music has storytelling lyrics, guitars, and influences from American folk and southern roots.

Blues

Blues is rooted in African-American history, known for expressive melodies and emotional lyrics.

Folk

Folk music emphasizes storytelling, acoustic instruments, and traditional melodies.

Metal

Metal is characterized by heavy riffs, powerful vocals, and intense drumming.

Funk

Funk emphasizes groove, rhythm, and often features strong basslines and rhythmic guitar.

Disco

Disco is dance-oriented, characterized by steady four-on-the-floor beats and lush instrumentals.

Folk Rock

Folk rock blends traditional folk melodies with rock instrumentation.

Synthwave

Synthwave draws inspiration from 80s synth music, with a futuristic-retro vibe.

Ambient

Ambient music focuses on atmosphere and mood, often instrumental and minimalistic.

Trap

Trap features hi-hats, booming bass, and rhythmic hi-hat rolls, popular in modern hip-hop.

Gospel

Gospel emphasizes strong vocals, spiritual lyrics, and soulful melodies.

Dancehall

Dancehall is a Jamaican genre characterized by rhythmic beats, offbeat basslines, and energetic vocals.

Bedroom Pop

Bedroom Pop is a lo-fi, intimate genre often created at home, emphasizing personal lyrics and relaxed melodies.
