Music Genres

Pop

Pop is a genre characterized by catchy melodies and hooks.

Hip-Hop

Hip-Hop features rhythmic speech and beats, often with poetic lyrics.

R&B

Rhythm and Blues focuses on soulful vocals and groove-oriented music.

Rock

Rock features electric guitars, drums, and energetic performances.

Electronic

Electronic music uses synthesizers and digital production techniques.

Jazz

Jazz is known for improvisation, complex chords, and swing rhythms.

Classical

Classical music involves intricate compositions, orchestras, and traditional instruments.

Reggae

Reggae features offbeat rhythms and socially conscious lyrics originating from Jamaica.

Country

Country music has storytelling lyrics, guitars, and influences from American folk and southern roots.

Blues

Blues is rooted in African-American history, known for expressive melodies and emotional lyrics.
